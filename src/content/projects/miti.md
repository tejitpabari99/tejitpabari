**The problem.** Motivational interviewing (MI) is a counseling style used to help people change health habits. Checking how well a session followed MI normally means a trained human coder, and that takes 1 to 2 weeks.

**What it does.** MITI gives your session a full evaluation using MITI 4.2.1, the standard coding system for MI. You get global ratings, behavior counts, summary scores and concrete tips, and every score links back to the exact transcript lines behind it.

**Who it's for.** Anyone who runs MI sessions and wants fast, trustworthy feedback on their own real conversations, not a practice simulator.

**How it's used.** Share your transcript, name the change goal of the session, and read the report while the session is still fresh.

**Status.** Live at miti.onolys.com, with early access open.
