**The problem.** Hospital bills are confusing, and errors are easy to miss.

**What it does.** Upload an itemized bill (PDF or photo). It checks the charges against public Medicare data and flags common problems: being billed for a more complex service than you got, services billed separately that belong together, drugs priced far above fair rates, and diagnosis codes that don't match the procedure.

**What you get.** A plain-language list of what looks wrong, plus a ready-to-send dispute letter.

**Why you can trust it.** Free and open source. No login, and your bill is not stored.
