Partner Event Finder is a guided helper for nonprofit staff. It looks at your partner organizations' public websites, finds their fundraising events, and keeps a shared calendar up to date.

## What it does

- Finds where each partner announces events such as galas, auctions and walks.
- Saves the links in a Google Sheet.
- Adds the events to a separate shared Google Calendar.
- Never changes your original list and never deletes anything.

## How a team uses it

1. Start with a list of your partner organizations.
2. Ask your AI assistant to find partner events. It walks you through setup in plain words.
3. It works in small rounds, starting with a sample of 5 organizations.
4. It tells you which events it added and gives you the link to the sheet.
5. Run it again every few months to catch new dates.

## What you get

- One calendar of upcoming partner events that the whole team can see.
- A faster way to decide which events staff should attend.
- No more checking dozens of websites by hand.
