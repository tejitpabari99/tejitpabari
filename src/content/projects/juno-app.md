**The problem.** Doctor visits are short and full of new information. It is easy to forget what to ask, or what was said.

**What it does.** Juno takes structured notes during the appointment, prompts useful questions you might not think of in the moment, and turns the conversation into a clear summary with concrete follow-ups.

**Who it's for.** Patients, especially those with complex, recurring conditions like MS, where you see specialists again and again.

**Built with experts.** Developed with neurologists and researchers. 200+ patients surveyed and 30+ doctors consulted along the way.
