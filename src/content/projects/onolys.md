**The problem.** Clinical assessments are only as good as the evidence behind them, and checking that by hand is slow and hard to repeat.

**What it does.** Onolys checks whether a written assessment faithfully represents the interview it came from, then produces an evidence audit you can review in a simple visual tool.

**First product.** MITI, a fast, evidence-linked evaluation of counseling sessions, is already live with early access open.

**Status.** Actively being built.
