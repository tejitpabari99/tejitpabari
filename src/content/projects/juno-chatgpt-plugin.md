**The problem.** Clinical notes are written for other clinicians. Patients are left with jargon and no clear idea of what to do next.

**What it does.** Give it a provider note (PDF, text, Word, web page or image) and it writes a structured care plan in plain language. Each answer is checked against the original note, and the final report shows its work.

**Who it's for.** Patients and caregivers who want to understand a note, not decode it.

**Privacy.** It runs on your own machine, with no server involved.
